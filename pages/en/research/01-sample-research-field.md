## 1. Introduction & Theoretical Framework
This study investigates computational methods for analyzing unstructured text data in scholarly research.

## 2. Methodology & Data Ingestion
- **Data Harvesting:** Unstructured texts harvested through open APIs and web scraping pipelines.
- **Preprocessing:** Normalization, tokenization, lemmatization, and stop-word filtering.
- **Model Training:** Topic modeling (LDA) and sentiment classification using supervised and unsupervised machine learning algorithms.

## 3. Conclusions & Reproducibility
Computational workflows enable researchers to categorize thousands of textual documents rapidly, offering transparent and reproducible alternatives to traditional manual content analysis.
