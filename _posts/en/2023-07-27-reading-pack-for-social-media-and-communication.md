### Course Reading Pack

This curated reading pack provides core literature, academic readings, and theoretical frameworks for the Social Media and Communication curriculum:

1. **The Evolution of Social Media:** Historical context of network society, user-generated content, algorithmic feeds, and digital platform dynamics.
2. **Theories of Media & Communication:** Harold Lasswell's linear models, Uses & Gratifications Theory, and critical cultural theory.
3. **Ethics in Digital Environments:** Algorithmic bias, fake news, fact-checking mechanisms, and the protection of digital human rights.
