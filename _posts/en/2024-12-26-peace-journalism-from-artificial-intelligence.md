Peace Journalism is defined as a form of journalism that fosters reconciliation between workers and unions, countries, or large groups. In this sector, an ethical approach is paramount. Journalists must provide accurate information, manage various pressures, uphold freedom of the press, and facilitate understanding of diverse cultural, ethnic, and political perspectives.

Peace journalism is a special type of socially responsible journalism that aims to contribute to the peaceful resolution of conflicts. This approach encourages the peaceful handling of not only physical but also social, cultural and religious differences. Journalists should prioritise plurality of voices and the principles of balanced and impartial journalism in their reporting and should not be a tool for violence, bullying and warmongering. In this way, it contributes to the establishment of a culture of peace in society and the harmonious coexistence of differences.
